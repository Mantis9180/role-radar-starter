# APM / PM / SPM research playbook

Candidate onboarding answers are authoritative. These baselines fill only missing judgment; they never override hard exclusions, location, work authorization, compensation, title, domain, or work-mode constraints.

## Common process

1. Re-verify every retained role on a live direct employer or ATS page.
2. Apply hard gates before scoring: actual minimum years, management expectations, technical/domain requirements, location, shifts, and authorization.
3. Rank newly posted or newly discovered roles first within each candidate-defined tier, then profile fit.
4. Recommend exactly one of the candidate's resume variants and one concrete tailoring action.
5. Mark funding and equity separately from fit. Future upside is inference; equity value is never guaranteed.
6. Preserve `firstSeenAt`; close stale listings instead of deleting them.

## APM baseline

Typical matches include Associate Product Manager, Junior Product Manager, Product Manager I, Product Analyst, Product Operations, and bounded Product Owner roles. Look for feature ownership, analytics, discovery support, launch execution, and learning velocity. Treat roles demanding independent portfolio strategy or long management experience as stretches or hard failures according to the listing.

## PM baseline

Typical matches include Product Manager, Product Manager I/II, Growth PM, Platform PM, Technical PM, AI PM, Marketplace PM, Product Owner, and Product Operations Manager. Look for end-to-end problem ownership, roadmap decisions, cross-functional leadership, business outcomes, and credible domain transfer.

## SPM baseline

Typical matches include Senior Product Manager, Lead Product Manager, Product Lead, Group Product Manager, Principal Product Manager, and senior technical/platform/growth roles. Inspect the actual mandate: portfolio scope, strategic influence, people leadership, executive communication, multi-squad coordination, and P&L expectations. Do not rank by title alone.

## Tier calibration

- P0: the candidate's highest-conviction, immediate applications. Require a strong fit and meaningful timing or asymmetric upside; funding alone is insufficient.
- P1: strong, credible fits with attractive scope, learning, brand, domain, or career compounding.
- P2: selective adjacent bets with a deliberate title, tenure, technical, domain, work-mode, or location stretch.
